Privacy Policy

Gunefod · Last updated October 26, 2026

Gunefod is a private iPhone app for keeping a personal shade archive: lens specifications you enter, inventory, wearing history, notes, photos, and generated archive images. This policy explains what stays on your device. By using Gunefod, you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Gunefod is published as a local iPhone application (bundle ID com.gunefod.archive). There is no Gunefod account and no Gunefod cloud that stores your archive. Records stay in this installation. For privacy questions, contact gunefod.archive.contact@gmail.com.

Information stored on this device

Depending on how you use the app, Gunefod may store the following in its private container on this device:

  • Photos you capture or choose, plus archive images the app generates from your entries.
  • Shade details, specifications, inventory counts, wearing dates, and notes you type or confirm after speech recognition.
  • Your remaining Archive Pass quantity and a local purchase ledger, including Apple transaction identifiers so the same purchase is not granted twice.
  • Display preferences such as whether you have finished the first-run screens.

Gunefod does not collect payment card numbers. Card details for in-app purchases are handled by Apple. The app does not request a location permission.

Camera, photos, voice, and notifications

Gunefod asks for a system permission only after you tap the related control. You may decline and continue with text entry or other tools that do not need that permission.

  • Camera — when you tap Take Photo to capture a picture for an entry.
  • Photos — when you tap Choose Photo, or when you save archive images into your photo library.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — when you tap Speak so spoken words can become notes. Audio is recorded to a temporary file on this device and deleted after transcription, cancellation, or failure. Gunefod does not keep a separate voice library.
  • Notifications — when you first open the home archive, the system may ask whether replacement reminders may be delivered. Reminders use dates you already saved on this device.

Speech recognition uses Apple’s on-device recognition when your selected language supports it. If on-device recognition is unavailable, Gunefod asks before recording whether you allow Apple’s online speech recognition. If you agree, Apple may process that audio under Apple’s privacy policy. You can decline and type instead. Gunefod does not request tracking permission and does not use advertising SDKs.

Purchases

Optional Archive Pass packs are consumable in-app purchases sold through Apple. Apple processes payment. Gunefod keeps verified transaction references and the remaining pass quantity locally. Unused passes stay on this installation and are removed if you delete the app or use Clear All Data. Gunefod does not offer a Restore Purchases control because passes are consumable and stored only on this device.

Sharing

Gunefod does not sell personal information and does not upload your archive to an advertising network. Shade records, photos, and voice audio are not sent to a third-party service operated by us. We do not share photos, audio, or voice recordings with a third-party analytics company.

Apple may receive purchase, device, notification, and speech-recognition information when you use App Store, StoreKit, system notifications, or online speech recognition after you allow it. If you save images to Photos, or if you back up this iPhone, those copies follow Apple’s Photos and backup rules. Opening this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use loads the page over HTTPS. The host may receive ordinary network information such as an IP address. That request does not include archive photos or the specifications you entered.

Retention and deletion

You can edit or delete individual entries and archive sets. Clear All Data removes the archive, media, history, and remaining passes from this installation. Photos you already saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them in Photos. Deleting the app also removes Gunefod’s local data. Device backups may still be handled by your operating system.

Children

Gunefod is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy. See also the Terms of Use.
